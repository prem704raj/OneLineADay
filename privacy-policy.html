Privacy Policy

One Line A Day

Last updated: August 31, 2026

1. Overview

One Line A Day is designed as a local-first journaling application. Your journal entries, moods, tags, attached media, and voice notes are primarily stored in the app's private storage on your device.

Important: We do not operate our own account or journal-sync server, and your private journal content is not intentionally sent to us for advertising or analytics. However, the app uses third-party Google services for advertising and purchases. Those services may process technical device and usage data.

2. Journal Data Stored on Your Device

Journal entries, moods, dates, tags, streak information, and related metadata are stored using Android Room and SQLite within application-private storage.

Photos, videos, and voice recordings that you attach may also be copied into private application storage.

One Line A Day does not currently add its own database-level encryption layer to the Room database. Android and your device may provide device-level storage encryption and security protections.

You may also enable App Lock, which uses Android's device authentication system.

3. Advertising and Google Services

One Line A Day uses Google Mobile Ads to display advertising, including banner, native, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements.

Google Mobile Ads may collect or share technical information such as:

  • IP address
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • App interactions
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Device or advertising identifiers

This information may be used for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and related Google services.

Journal text, attached journal photos, journal tags, and voice recordings are not intentionally supplied to Google Mobile Ads for advertising.

The application also uses Google Play Billing for optional premium purchases. Google Play handles payment processing. The app may receive purchase and entitlement information required to determine whether premium features are available. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

Google's privacy information is available at Google Privacy Policy .

4. Device Permissions

One Line A Day may use the following Android permissions:

  • Notifications: Optional daily journal reminders.
  • Microphone: Used only when you choose to record a voice note.
  • Receive Boot Completed: Used to restore scheduled reminders after a device restart.
  • Biometric authentication: Used for the optional App Lock feature.
  • Internet and network state: Required by services including advertising and Google Play.

The application does not request general location, contacts, SMS, call-log, or broad external-storage permissions.

5. Backup, Export and Sharing

Android system backup or device-transfer functionality may back up eligible application database and preference data when those features are enabled on your device or account.

One Line A Day also provides user-initiated export features. If you export a backup, PDF, image, or other journal file and choose another application or cloud-storage service, the exported data will be handled by the service you select.

We do not automatically upload your journal entries to our own cloud server.

6. Security

Journal data is stored within Android application-private storage. The application also provides an optional App Lock using Android device authentication.

No application or security system can guarantee absolute security. We recommend protecting your Android device with a PIN, password, pattern, or supported biometric authentication.

7. Children's Privacy

One Line A Day is not specifically designed as a service directed at children.

We do not claim that the application is automatically compliant with every child-privacy law worldwide. Additional requirements may apply depending on the target audience, country, advertising configuration, and distribution settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features, third-party SDKs, legal requirements, or data practices change. The latest revision date will appear at the top of this page.
